Our Mission

Management Information Systems Business Association

MISBA is the premier student organization for individuals interested in the intersection of Management, Information Systems, and Business Analytics at the University of Minnesota Duluth.

We provide members with opportunities to develop professional skills, network with regional business leaders, and explore the rapidly evolving landscape of technology in the corporate world.
